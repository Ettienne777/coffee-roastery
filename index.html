Our Story

Rooted in Limpopo.
Raised by fire.
Crafted with purpose.

Golden Boabab Coffee Roasting was born in Naboomspruit, Limpopo — a place of rich soil, warm people and wide-open skies. We believe great coffee doesn't start in a factory. It starts in the land, in the hands that pick it, and in the fire that brings it to life.

This is more than coffee.
This is our story.
